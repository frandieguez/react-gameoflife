## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The board is a specimen under observation, not a screen — refusing both the neon-hacker-terminal costume (what this project already wore) and a pastel edtech-cute opposite. Conway's cells are colonies growing and dying on a lit culture plate.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm backlit amber/cream agar ground (never black); live cells glow as bioluminescent teal-green colonies built from flat layered opacity bands, never a soft blurred gradient; empty wells stay faintly etched/visible even when no cell lives there; generation/speed readouts sit on masking-tape lab labels; fine registration crosshairs mark the dish's four corners; a slow-rotating circular "specimen" stamp sits by the label; the whole plate breathes (a slow ambient pulse) while running.

**STORY:** A visitor opens the link and finds a lab bench moment already in progress — a plate seeded, labeled, lit — then presses the incubator toggle and watches colonies bloom and die in real time.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** An incubator lamp flickers on over a dark bench, then the dish (seeded with the glider) resolves into view under full light; label, crosshairs, and stamp settle in after the plate itself is visible. Primary action (play) sits in the control strip below the plate.

**FORM:** Candidate 3 of the grounded direction list ("Petri dish / microbiology culture plate under a scope"), seed key `c6986f96`.
